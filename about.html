Our Story

Built on green lawns, run on old-fashioned attention.

Founded in 1990 on GT Road, Krishna International School has stayed a CBSE co-educational school of manageable size — by choice, not by accident.

Mission

Academics and character, not academics or character.

The school is committed to promoting sound moral values and a scientific temperament alongside exam results. Regular counselling gives students insight into the competitive world ahead while encouraging a healthy — not anxious — spirit of competition.

Under the motto "Dedicated to Excellence," staff are expected to help students compete for professional seats while also building social awareness, pride in cultural heritage, and a sense of responsibility toward the country.

Vision

A school that fits in a teacher's memory, not just a spreadsheet.

At just over a thousand students across fourteen year-groups, class sizes stay small enough that a teacher can name a struggling student's specific gap — not just their grade.

That scale is deliberate: it's the reason the 1:15 teacher-student ratio has held steady even as demand for seats has grown.

Our Teachers

The real strength of the school is its faculty.

Teachers here shape more than test scores — with their knowledge and experience, they shape the habits students carry into every stage after school. K.I.S. teachers are qualified and trained against international standards of education, not just state board minimums.

01

Subject specialists

Senior classes are taught by subject-qualified teachers rather than generalists covering multiple boards.

02

Regular counselling

Structured counselling sessions help students navigate stream choices and competitive exam pressure early.

03

Continuous monitoring

Performance is tracked through the term, not just at report card time, so gaps are caught while they're small.

Campus & Facilities

Away from the city's pollution, built for focus.
