CBSE Curriculum

Fourteen year-groups. One consistent standard.

From Play Group through Class XII, the same emphasis carries through every stage: concept before memorization, and steady assessment over last-minute cramming.

PG – I

Foundation Years

Early childhood learning built around play, storytelling, and guided discovery rather than seat-based instruction.

  • Play-based learning and motor skill development
  • Early language exposure and phonics
  • Basic numeracy through games and objects
  • Social interaction and turn-taking
II – V

Primary School

Core academic skills are built here — reading fluency, arithmetic, and the classroom habits that carry through senior years.

  • Reading and writing skill-building
  • Fundamental mathematics
  • Introductory science concepts
  • Moral education and classroom discipline
VI – VIII

Middle School

Analytical thinking takes over from rote learning, with more responsibility placed on the student.

  • Concept-based mathematics and science
  • Social studies and current-affairs awareness
  • Structured presentation and communication practice
  • First introduction to competitive exam formats
IX – XII

Senior School

Board-exam preparation runs alongside stream counselling, so choices for XI and XII are made with real information, not guesswork.

  • CBSE board-aligned subject teaching
  • Regular internal assessments and doubt-clearing sessions
  • Science, Commerce, and Humanities stream options
  • Career and competitive-exam counselling
How We Teach

Concept-based, not memorization-based.

01

Interactive discussion

Classes are structured around questions, not one-way lectures.

02

Activity-based learning

Labs and group activities turn theory into something students have actually done.

03

Continuous assessment

Frequent, low-stakes checks replace one high-stakes exam per term.
