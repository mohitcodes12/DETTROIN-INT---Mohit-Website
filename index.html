Why K.I.S.

Away from the city's pace, built for a child's pace.

Set amid green lawns off GT Road, the campus was designed so students learn without the pressure of city pollution and noise — with a stacked library, working laboratories, and playing fields all on one site.

Under the school motto "Dedicated to Excellence," teachers are asked to do two jobs at once: build academic competence, and build the social awareness and responsibility that competence should serve.

Academic Journey

One school, every stage — Play Group to Class XII.

Board affiliation: CBSE. Co-educational, English medium, with a curriculum that scales in depth rather than switching philosophy at every stage.

PG – I

Foundation

Play-based learning, early language, and first exposure to numbers through activity, not worksheets.

II – V

Primary

Reading fluency, foundational mathematics, and the classroom habits that carry through senior years.

VI – VIII

Middle

Concept-based science and math, plus the first structured debate and presentation work.

IX – XII

Senior

Board-exam preparation with regular assessment, doubt-clearing sessions, and stream counselling.
